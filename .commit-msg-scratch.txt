fix(vtt): fix three real causes of the persistent no-mesh-derivable path-brush error

A random-stress test (many overlapping strokes over a real terrain grid) was
added to session.rs specifically to chase the user's consistently-reproducible
"no mesh derivable for ...-remainder" report, since every earlier targeted
synthetic repro had applied cleanly. It found three separate, real defects on
the very first run:

1. triangulate_region (surface-mesh) aborted the WHOLE region's mesh if a
   single hole did not nest inside exactly one outer loop. A remainder region
   commonly has several disjoint outer loops at once (several unrelated,
   non-adjacent surfaces consumed by the same stroke, never sharing an edge to
   collapse into one ring), and the new stroke contour used as that region's
   hole very often spans across more than one of them, or lands outside all
   of them. That must not fail the entire region's mesh -- every other,
   cleanly-owned piece still has to render. An unresolvable hole is now simply
   dropped (that one piece renders as its own full, unnotched loop) instead of
   the whole triangulate_region call returning None.

2. round_arc (analytic_brush, the fast path for a single-arc stroke with a
   circle brush) fails whenever the brush radius exceeds the arc's own turning
   radius (a tight turn with a fat brush -- inner_radius goes negative). The
   earlier i_overlay union-based fix only routed the non-circle-brush arc case
   into that fallback; round_arc's own internal failure for this case was
   never caught and routed there too. Now it is: round_arc failing falls back
   to union_stroke_footprint, same as every other self-overlap case.

3. BrushShapeRequest's square/hexagon variants demanded literal snake_case
   rotation_radians on the wire. serde's rename_all on an internally-tagged
   enum only renames variant names, not each variant's own field names -- it
   does not recurse into struct-variant fields. Every real caller (the TS
   BrushShape type, unchanged) sends camelCase rotationRadians, so every
   square or hexagon path-brush stroke failed to even parse. Fixed with an
   explicit #[serde(rename = "rotationRadians")] per field.

New tests: many_random_overlapping_strokes_never_leave_an_unmeshable_surface
(deterministic xorshift stress test, 80 strokes over a 6x6 terrain grid,
mixing shapes/positions/sample counts, re-derives the mesh for exactly the
keys the front end itself re-fetches after every single stroke),
square_and_hexagon_shapes_parse_camel_case_rotation_from_the_wire.
